# THE MISSING SIGNATURE — Director's Treatment / Production Bible v1.0

**形式：** 60s / 1080×1920 / 30fps / English voiceover with Traditional Chinese subtitles.  
**Genre：** cinematic cyber-thriller, precise technological realism.  
**Status：** script and storyboard only; no video has been rendered.  
**Fact source：** https://blog.faav.net/how-i-couldve-accessed-17-trillion-microsoft-records (Sep 25, 2026).

## 1. Logline and editorial integrity

An apparently locked Microsoft internal dashboard concealed a reachable API. An AI agent found an unsigned-JWT vulnerability but stalled at the user lookup; its sixteen-year-old researcher tried `admin` and obtained a working query, exposing a possible path into an immense analytics estate. The flaw was responsibly disclosed and the endpoint was restricted. The movie is about **failure to verify cryptographic proof**, not a data-dump heist.

This is a dramatization, not footage of Titan or actual Microsoft interfaces. **17.3 trillion rows is an estimated metadata-derived potential reach, NOT downloaded or leaked records.** Use the label `ESTIMATED / POTENTIALLY REACHABLE / NOT EXFILTRATED` on S09. Do not reproduce employee records, real tokens, endpoints, or credentials. The observable result `1` is from `SELECT 1`, not the amount of breached data.

## 2. Story spine and psychological intent

- **Act I, 00:00–00:29.5 — contradiction and mounting anxiety:** The closed frontend versus publicly reachable API; ten days of error-driven exploration; empty cryptographic signature slot.
- **Act II, 00:29.5–00:46.5 — human hypothesis, micro-payoff, macro-scale:** a single `admin` identity mapping; quiet query result `1`; explosive scale represented without false leak imagery.
- **Act III, 00:46.5–01:00 — responsible disclosure and unresolved aftertaste:** MSRC report, restriction and award; end by matching the opening image and emphasizing the missing signature verification.

The high-retention hypotheses to test, not facts: visual contradiction in first 4s; a prolonged pause immediately before the `admin` reveal; aesthetic quiet instead of alarms when `1` appears; a readable 3-second-scale data estimate; final ring composition to encourage replay.

## 3. Cinematography and art direction

One central motif: **the same security door in the first and final frame**. Never use green Matrix rain, random hexadecimal code, exaggerated red ALERT overlays, fake Microsoft employee names, or authentic-looking internal screenshots. All information text remains face-on, pixel-accurate DOM/SVG typography. Reserve Three.js for geometries that truly need depth: vault, side port, floating JWT glass, room, and data architecture.

- **Camera language**: deliberate dolly/orbit for suspense, a true hard cut on `admin → 1`, acceleration then deceleration on the data-reveal pullback. Prefer 35, 50, 85mm full-frame-equivalent *visual feel* (not literal Three.js lens settings).
- **Light**: 5:1 ratio inspired noir, cool rim light, soft fog controlled. A single tungsten/amber accent marks human inference. Strong depth separation but readable dark values on phones.
- **Palette**: near-black `#070B14`, dark navy `#111D2C`, steel cyan `#74B8C6`, restrained amber `#E0A96B`, rejection red `#C85A5A`, white `#F1F4F6`.
- **Texture**: subtle film grain and slight vignette; no extreme chromatic aberration, smear, fake camera shake, distorted technical labels.
- **Motion**: continuous match movements where possible; use 0.3s scene opening buffers when useful, and no more than two large focus objects per shot. Final 0.4s holds near silence.
- **Titles and captions**: large bold titles, short high-contrast words; zh-TW captions maximum two lines. Set text via 2D compositing, NEVER perspective-map essential captions into Three.js objects.
- **Portrait-safe house bounds**: target critical text / faces within x=100..890 and y=250..1550 on 1080×1920, plus manual YouTube UI checks. This is a conservative in-house guide, not an official platform guarantee.

## 5. Complete English narration

> Microsoft's front door said: VPN required. But a public API was still listening. An AI agent found the lead for a sixteen-year-old researcher. For ten days, it chased error messages, one security check at a time. The server inspected the token's claims—but never verified its signature. Every normal-looking username failed. Then the researcher tried one word: admin. The query returned one. Beyond it were seventeen databases, with an estimated seventeen point three trillion stored rows potentially reachable. He checked limited samples, then reported the flaw. Microsoft restricted the endpoint, and awarded five thousand dollars. It checked the claims. Not the signature.

Word-count should be checked after final copy. Aim for a restrained US English voice, around 120–135 WPM overall with actual held silences; timecodes are edit targets, not a license to speed-stretch speech. Do not automatically apply the faster 1.4× CEH teaching voice profile: this standalone suspense short should be a distinct `cinematic-short` voice preset, without altering the existing CEH configuration.

## 6. Sound design (precise cues)

- **0–12s**: soft -noise floor, single mechanical thud, weak right-side network ping; dry close-mic narrator, avoid loud intro logo.
- **12–22s**: three consistent diagnostic ticks lead to a rising low harmonic tension bed.
- **22–24.5s**: rhythmic motif drops out on `signature`; leave an audible negative-space moment.
- **28.7–31.3s**: music ducks toward near-silence; include a roughly 0.65s near-total gap just before the final typed word `admin`.
- **34s**: one weighted, subdued impact under numeral `1`; no triumph stinger or alarm.
- **37–46.5s**: sub-bass expansion without clipping; let the giant number remain legible a minimum of ~3s, with music ducked during text explanation.
- **46.5–56s**: clean and measured resolution; subtle latch on the restricted endpoint.
- **56–60s**: return to the initial mechanical timbre, tail fades into near silence so first/last imagery can loop.

Audio source deliverables: unprocessed 48 kHz PCM voice WAV (dry master), individual cue stems, music stem with license/source log, final mix. Working release checks: target around -14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP (engineering guidelines, not a promise or platform requirement); audible VO and no harsh sibilance.

## 7. Implementation architecture in existing pipeline

Preserve the existing Python + GSAP + HyperFrames + FFmpeg system; add a 9:16 project profile instead of changing the CEH 16:9 project settings. Keep a dedicated shot-manifest (`shotlist_60s.json`) and deterministic `frame = round(seconds × 30)` anchors.

| Job | Technology | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Door, glass JWT, 17 data towers, dolly/camera | Three.js | Geometric materials, physically credible lighting; seeded motion, time-indexed state |
| Exact captions, claim labels, numeric scale, diagrams | HTML/CSS/SVG + GSAP | Flat, sharp, constraint-based placement; no distorted typography |
| Track editing, timing, scene orchestration, final encode | HyperFrames + FFmpeg | Composition clips and audio; seekable timeline |
| Voice/TTS and timing | Existing Python/TTS pipeline | Record voice before locking final scene boundaries; align captions to measured phoneme/word times |
| Visual quality checks | Playwright screenshots, screenshot diff, ffprobe | Test critical frames, caption safe regions, deterministic seeks, A/V parity |

**Important adapter status:** HyperFrames documentation currently shows GSAP first-party adapter as available and Three.js/WebGL first-party adapter as planned, while prompting docs suggest Three via an adapter. Do not assume native adapter exists in your installed version. Implement a tested custom `seekFrame(frame)` Three.js wrapper if supported, or **pre-render each Three.js shot as deterministic local video frames/MP4** and import these clips into HyperFrames, retaining DOM/SVG overlays. No wall-clock animation, `requestAnimationFrame` dependence, unseeded `Math.random()`, or network requests at render time. `renderAt(t)` must derive 100% of camera and material state from canonical `t = frame / fps`.

Use local files and vetted asset licenses; never distribute font files through final deliverables. Add image/geometry licensing log; avoid copying copyrighted movie sets for direct release.

## 8. Stage-gated execution

1. **Script lock:** approve every technical claim and narration sentence against original source.
2. **Animatic:** make still/layout preview for all twelve shots, plus 10-second proof covering the silent `admin → 1` reveal; do not build giant database architecture until narrative timing works.
3. **Sound lock:** render each narrator voice clip once, measure exact clip times, allocate silences. Subtitle timecodes must be revised from actual voice, not arbitrary shot positions.
4. **Scene implementation:** true 3D only when justified; all essential text via SVG/HTML with layout tokens; fix lens and color treatment; reuse door asset between opening and closing.
5. **Technical QA:** random-seek and repeat-seek frame comparisons (e.g. 90→10→50→10); scene-boundary first/last-frame checks; verify count 1,800 frames at 30fps, 1080×1920; audio existence and exact duration; no text clipping or unsafe bottom-right placement.
6. **Factual QA:** 17.3T always accompanied by ESTIMATED / POTENTIALLY REACHABLE; no '17 trillion hacked or stolen'; no invented screenshot; include research attribution in video description.
7. **Distribution QA:** review sound-on and muted mobile previews; have three testers retell the failure chain after one view. Revise hook/legibility on actual feedback, not assumed retention metrics.
