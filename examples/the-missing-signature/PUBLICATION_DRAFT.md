## Title

A 16-Year-Old Found Microsoft's Missing Signature

## Description

Researcher Faav describes finding a signature-verification flaw in Microsoft's Titan analytics API in 2026. His AI agent Antares found the lead; the human insight was recognizing a local username lookup. The researcher reported limited verification and an estimated 17.3 trillion stored rows potentially reachable through the service. That total was not a mass download. According to his coordinated disclosure, Microsoft restricted the endpoint and awarded a $5,000 bounty.

Visuals are dramatizations. The 17 towers represent connected analytics databases; their heights do not encode measured quantities. English narration is synthetic.

Source: https://blog.faav.net/how-i-couldve-accessed-17-trillion-microsoft-records
