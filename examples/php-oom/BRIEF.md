## Adopted direction
Hollywood cyber-thriller × scientific visualization. Suspense → discovery → escalation → revelation → resolution. One vulnerability per film. Genuine Three.js geometry, macro perspective, motivated tracking, rack focus, restrained orbit and match cuts. No anonymous hacker montage, fake terminals, automatic root access or implication that all SAPIs share identical exploitability.

## Gates
1. Source-backed script, design and measured timeline.
2. Five rendered keyframes, internally inspected and revised.
3. Complete voice, score, sound effects, animation and MP4.
4. Independent source, scene, text, audio, encoded-video and archive checks.
5. One final user review of both complete films.
